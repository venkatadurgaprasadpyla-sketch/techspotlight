The Aero 14 is a made-up laptop, but it stands in for the kind of thin-and-light machine most
readers in India shortlist: around ₹75,000, light enough for a daily commute and quiet enough
for a library.

## Design and build

The sample chassis weighs 1.2 kg and flexes very little when you lift it by one corner. The lid
opens with one hand.

## Display

A 14-inch 2.8K OLED panel is the highlight. It is bright enough to use near a window and its
colours look natural out of the box.

## Performance

For browsers, office apps and light photo edits, the Aero 14 never felt slow in this sample
review. It is not built for long gaming sessions.

## Battery life

In our sample test it lasted 13 hours 24 minutes, the longest of any laptop in this sample set.

## The ups

Battery life, weight and silence make this an easy laptop to live with.

## The downs

The webcam is soft in dim light and the speakers distort at full volume.
