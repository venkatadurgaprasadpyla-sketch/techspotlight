On 13 October, iQOO's India CEO Nipun Marya confirmed that the iQOO 16 is coming to India. Asked when, he said, "Soon." There is no official price yet.

Let's separate what we know from what we don't.

**What iQOO has confirmed:** a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip, a Samsung 2K display at 165Hz, and a battery above 8,000mAh. The software is expected to be OriginOS 7, though details were still being finalised. In China the phone launched on 29 September at CNY 5,999. Smartprix converts that to about ₹86,000, before India's duties and taxes.

**What we don't know:** the India price, the launch date, which AI features will come, camera performance, and how many years of updates iQOO will promise. Online price guesses vary. Some say above ₹1 lakh. That is a guess, not a fact.

Here is the bigger picture. Smartprix, citing Counterpoint, says global smartphone shipments fell about 20 percent year on year as memory chip costs rose and supply tightened. When parts cost more, brands tend to pass it on. Mr Marya himself said, "The definition of competitive pricing has changed."

The same pattern may show up elsewhere. The OPPO Find X10 is expected in India from late October, with reports pointing to a base price above ₹90,000. The vivo X500 series may follow in late October or November. These India prices are expectations, not announcements.

A fast chip is only the start of a good flagship. What decides whether a phone still feels premium after a few weeks is how it holds its speed when it gets warm, how long the battery lasts, how steady the camera is in low light, and how polished the software feels. iQOO says it wants the iQOO 16 judged on all of these, not just gaming. That is a fair goal, and one we can only check once reviews arrive.

## What this means for you

**If you are planning to buy a flagship this festive season:** Don't pay for a guess. Wait for the official India price and the first reviews. In a few weeks you will know the real price, and you can compare the iQOO 16 with the OPPO Find X10 and the vivo X500.

**If your current phone still works well:** You are not behind. Phones are lasting longer. A good battery replacement may give you another year.

**If you want to buy something now:** Last year's flagships and strong mid-rangers often drop in price when new ones arrive. Check Amazon.in and Flipkart for the final price, bank offers and exchange value, since these change daily.

**If you care about updates:** Ask how many years of updates the phone gets. At this price, I would want a clear promise.

**Still unclear:** whether higher prices are temporary, and how iQOO will price its different storage variants.
