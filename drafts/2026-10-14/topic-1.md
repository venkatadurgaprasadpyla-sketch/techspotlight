Apple's first foldable iPhone is almost here. Pre-orders open on 16 October and the phone goes on sale on 23 October.

Let's start with the number that most of us will look at first. In India, the iPhone Duo starts at ₹2,99,900 for 256GB. The 512GB model is ₹3,24,900, the 1TB model is ₹3,74,900 and the 2TB model is ₹4,49,900. In the UAE it starts at Dh8,499. In the US it starts at $1,999, and one Indian report says the Indian price is more than ₹1 lakh above the US price once you convert it.

That is a lot of money. So what do you get?

**The shape is the story.** Closed, the phone has a 5.4-inch outer screen, about the size of a passport. Open it, and you get a 7.6-inch inner display, the biggest screen Apple has put on an iPhone. Apple says apps can use the bigger space, and the outer screen works as a preview when you photograph someone. There are two colours: Star White and Night Sky.

**Software matters here too.** The phone runs Siri AI, Apple's new version of its assistant, and Apple says it will ship with an iOS 27.1 update ready on day one. In the UAE, the phone is eSIM-only. There is no physical SIM tray. If you still carry a physical SIM, check this with your operator before you pre-order.

**What we don't know yet.** Sources disagree on the battery size and the camera setup, so I won't repeat numbers I can't confirm. We also don't have independent reviews yet. Nobody outside Apple can tell you how the crease looks after a month, or how the hinge feels after a year. That is the part of any foldable that only time answers.

## What this means for you

**If you are in India:** At ₹2,99,900 this is a luxury purchase. Apple lists EMI and instant cashback options, but check the final terms on Apple's page and your bank's card offers. Pre-orders open at 5:30 PM IST on 16 October. If you are happy with your current iPhone, there is no reason to rush. Reviews will arrive within days of sale.

**If you are in the UAE:** The starting price is Dh8,499, and pre-orders open on 16 October. Reports differ on the exact time and on whether some retailers opened early, so check Apple AE and your retailer directly. Remember the eSIM-only point above.

**If you are an iPhone 15 or 16 user:** You are not missing a must-have upgrade. A foldable is a different kind of phone, not just a better one. Ask yourself whether you want a bigger screen in your pocket, or just a good phone.

**A simple rule I follow:** with a first-generation product at this price, I would wait for real reviews before paying.
