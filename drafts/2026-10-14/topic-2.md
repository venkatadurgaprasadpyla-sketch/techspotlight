Apple used its "Welcome home" event on 13 October to show three new home products. The names are HomeView, a new Apple TV 4K and the HomePod mini 2. There was also a Pokémon edition of the Beats Pill, but let's stay with the home products.

**HomeView** is a smart home screen with a 7-inch display, a speaker base and Siri AI. It comes as a tabletop model and a wall-mounted model. It can recognise up to six people in the house and show each person their own home screen. It handles music, video calls, timers and recipes, and it can show up to nine camera feeds at once. In the US, reports put the price at $299. It goes on sale on 13 November. Apple says it is not available in the EU. I could not find an India or UAE price for it yet.

**The new Apple TV 4K** has an A19 chip, Wi-Fi 7, a Siri Remote you can find with Find My, and microphones that let you talk to Siri without holding a button. In India it costs ₹39,900 for the 128GB model and arrives on 13 November.

**HomePod mini 2** costs $149 in the US and ₹18,900 in India, according to Smartprix. It arrives on 30 October. One detail to know: Apple says it will not support Siri AI right away.

Here's what stood out to me. These are not three separate gadgets. Apple is building one idea, a home that Siri AI runs from room to room. That idea gets expensive quickly. Smartprix points out that four HomePod mini 2 speakers (₹75,600) plus the Apple TV (₹39,900) cost more than most good soundbars.

## What this means for you

**If you are in India:** The Apple TV 4K price jumped. Smartprix notes that the 2022 model cost ₹16,900. If your current Apple TV works well, there is little reason to change. The one feature many people will like is Find My on the remote. India is also among the first eight countries for the Siri AI beta, in English only for now, so check what works on your device before you count on it.

**If you are in the UAE:** I could not confirm UAE prices or whether Siri AI will be available at launch. I would check Apple AE before you plan a purchase.

**If you own HomeKit gadgets today:** HomeView can run alongside your iPhone and iPad, and Apple says it will work with partner accessories from brands like LG and Schneider Electric. Check that your lights, locks and cameras are supported before you buy a hub.

**What we don't know yet:** How well Siri AI works in everyday Indian homes, and how HomeView feels after a few months. No one has reviewed these products yet. I will keep watching.
